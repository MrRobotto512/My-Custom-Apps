Display panels

Full-screen panels for dead / stuck pixels, colour uniformity, backlight bleed and lines. Best in a dim room. Click or press any key for the next panel, Esc to stop.

red · green · blue · white · black · 50% grey · gradient · 1-px grid · checker · text
Look for: bright/dark dots that do not move · a tint on one side of white · glow at the edges on black · vertical/horizontal lines on grey · banding on the gradient · moiré or a dark patch on the grid · blurry text (bad panel or wrong resolution).

Result

Flicker / PWM: film the grey panel with a phone camera at 1/1000 s — rolling bars = backlight driver. Dead pixels: Apple's service threshold is model-specific; note the count and where.

Keyboard—

Press every key. A key turns green when it registers, red if it double-types (chatter). Hold fn for the top row (macOS eats the brightness/volume keys otherwise). ⌘Q / ⌘W / ⌘Tab cannot be blocked by a web page — press ⌘ on its own. Touch ID / power key cannot be tested here.

Trackpad

Move the pointer over every zone, click, two-finger click (right-click), scroll both ways, drag a line, double-click, and force click (Safari only). Zones go green as the pointer crosses them — a zone that never lights is a dead spot.

Speakers

Set the Mac's volume to about 60 % first. Listen for a dead channel, crackle, rattle (blown cone / debris) or a buzz at low frequencies.

levelidle
Sweep: a dip or rattle at one frequency = damaged cone or loose grille. Bass: buzz = blown woofer / debris under the speaker (liquid). One side silent with the balance centred = speaker or amp on that side, or a top-case flex.
